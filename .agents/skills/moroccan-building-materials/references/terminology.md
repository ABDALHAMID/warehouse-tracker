# Moroccan Construction Yard Terminology & Darija Glossary
# (معجم مصطلحات وكلمات ديبو مواد البناء بالمغرب)

In Morocco, construction yard workers, master builders (*L'mâallem* / المعلم), contractors, and truck drivers use a blend of Moroccan Darija, French, and specialized artisan vocabulary. This glossary explains key terminology.

---

## 1. General Yard Vocabulary

| Darija Term | Script | French Term | Meaning / Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dépôt / Dibo | ديبو | Dépôt de matériaux | Building materials warehouse / yard |
| L'mâallem | المعلم | Maître artisan / Chef maçon | Master builder, plasterer, or tiling craftsman |
| Slâa | السلعة | Marchandise / Matériaux | Goods, inventory, raw materials |
| Charijo / Charget | شارجي | Charger / Chargement | Load materials onto a truck or pickup |
| Dégaji / Dechargé | ديشارجي | Décharger | Unload materials into warehouse bays |
| Bon / Reçu | البون | Bon de livraison / Facture | Delivery note, dispatch slip, receipt |
| Camionnette / Honda | هوندا / بيكوب | Triporteur / Pick-up | Light delivery pickup (Honda, Isuzu, Hyundai) |
| Remorque | الرموك | Semi-remorque | Heavy truck delivering bulk cement or steel coils |
| Dala | الدالة | Dalle de compression / Plancher | Concrete floor slab |
| Poteau / Sari | السارية / البوطو | Poteau / Colonne | Reinforced concrete pillar |
| Fondasyone | الفونداسيون | Fondations / Semelles | Ground foundations and footings |
| Mortoub | المرطوب | Enduit de façade / Crépi | Cement mortar wall render / plastering |

## 2. Category 1: Liants & Granulats (Ciment & Granulats)

| Darija Term | French Equivalent | Notes & Commercial Brands |
|---|---|---|
| **Sima 45** (السيما 45) | Ciment CPJ 45 | High-strength gray cement (LafargeHolcim, Asment Témara, Ciments du Maroc). Used for structural concrete. |
| **Sima 35** (السيما 35) | Ciment CPJ 35 | Standard masonry cement. Used for bricklaying and mortar render. |
| **Sima l-bida** (السيما البيضا) | Ciment blanc | White cement. Used for tiling joints, marble, and decorative work. |
| **Ramla d-l'oued** (الرملة د الواد) | Sable de rivière / d'oued | Washed river sand for high-quality mortar. |
| **Ramla mghorbla** (الرملة مغربلة) | Sable tamisé | Sifted sand without stones, ready for plastering. |
| **Ghyas / Kayass** (الكياس) | Gravette concassée | Crushed gravel (G1: 15/25mm, G2). |
| **Gebs** (القبص) | Plâtre de construction | Moroccan building plaster for mouldings, drywall, and ceilings. |
| **Khencha** (خنشة) | Sac de 50kg / 25kg / 40kg | Traditional Moroccan word for heavy woven or kraft paper sacks. |

## 3. Category 2: Aciers & Armatures (الحديد)

| Darija Term | French Equivalent | Notes & Trade Conventions |
|---|---|---|
| **L-h'did d 8 / Rwayza** (الرويزة د 8) | Fer Ø8mm | 8mm rebar, historically nicknamed "Rwayza". Used for stirrups (*cadres*). |
| **L-h'did d 10** (الحديد د 10) | Fer Ø10mm | 10mm rebar. Used for standard residential columns. |
| **L-h'did d 12** (الحديد د 12) | Fer Ø12mm | 12mm rebar. Primary reinforcement for floor beams and slabs. |
| **L-h'did d 14 / 16** (الحديد د 14 و 16) | Fer Ø14mm / Ø16mm | Heavy rebars for foundation footings and large structural spans. |
| **Barra** (بارة) | Barre de fer 12m | Standard commercial 12-meter straight steel bar (Sonasid, Univers Acier). |
| **Selk l-merbout** (سلك المربوط) | Fil de recuit / ligature | Soft annealed black tie wire (1.2mm) to tie rebar cages together. |
| **Krouma / Treillis** (الكرومة) | Treillis soudé | Welded wire mesh sheets for topping slabs. |

## 4. Category 3: Briques & Parpaings (الباجور والبارفان)

| Darija Term | French Equivalent | Notes & Commercial Formats |
|---|---|---|
| **Yajour d 6** (الياجور د 6) | Brique 6 trous (7x15x25) | Light hollow red brick for interior partition walls (*cloisons*). |
| **Yajour d 8** (الياجور د 8) | Brique 8 trous (10x15x25) | Standard hollow red clay brick for exterior and room walls. |
| **Bajour d 12** (الباجور د 12) | Brique 12 trous (15x20x25) | Heavy double-chamber clay brick for sound and thermal insulation. |
| **Barfane d 15 / 20** (البارفان) | Parpaing / Aggloméré | Hollow concrete vibration block. Very sturdy, used for perimeter walls. |
| **Ourdi / Hourdis** (الوردي) | Entrevous / Hourdis | Hollow concrete or clay block placed between precast concrete joists (*poutrelles*). |

## 5. Category 4: Bois & Coffrage (العود والكوفراج)

| Darija Term | French Equivalent | Notes & Dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| **Blancha d 4m** (البلانشة) | Planche de coffrage | White pine wood board (400 x 10 x 2.5 cm) for column and beam formwork. |
| **Madri** (المادري) | Madrier pin | Heavy thick timber beam (4m x 15cm x 7.5cm) to support heavy slab loads. |
| **Chevrone** (الشفرون) | Chevron | Square timber batten (4m x 6cm x 8cm). |
| **Contreplaké filmé** (كونطربلاكي فيلمي) | Contreplaqué bakélisé | Waterproof smooth phenolic film-faced plywood (18mm) for fair-faced concrete. |
| **Siroute** (السيروت) | Étai métallique | Adjustable telescopic steel prop to support floor formwork until concrete cures. |
